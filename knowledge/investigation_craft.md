# Investigation craft

## Budget discipline
Expansion is the scarce resource. Expand where the money went, not where it came from, unless sourcing is the question. Contracts, exchanges and mixers are usually terminal: record them as findings and spend budget on unexplained intermediate wallets instead.

## Reading structure
Pass-through ratio near 1 with a short activity span marks a conduit. High fan-in with a long history marks a service. One-time counterparties receiving round amounts suggest payouts. Depth matters: a suspicious pattern two hops from the seed is case-relevant; five hops out it is usually someone else's story.

## Writing the case
Lead with what the money did: amounts, dates, direction. Confidence comes from converging independent signals, not from any single label. Every claim should name the addresses it rests on, and the report should say explicitly what was not established.
